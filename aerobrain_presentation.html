⚡ The Solution

What AeroBrain Actually Does

AeroBrain is not a chatbot. It is an intelligence layer that sits between your team and the chaotic aviation aftermarket. Here's the exact workflow:

1

📩 User Asks in Natural Language

"Find me a CFM56-7B26 engine fan blade, serviceable, with 2,500+ cycles remaining, under $12,000."
No dropdowns. No part number lookups. Just talk.

2

🧠 AI Parses & Enriches the Intent

AeroBrain deconstructs the query into structured parameters (part family, condition, price ceiling, cycle limits), cross-references ATA classifications, and flags any ambiguities before searching.

3

🔍 Multi-Source Intelligence Sweep

Simultaneously queries internal inventory, ILS/PartsBase feeds, teardown forecast data, and historical pricing to build a complete market picture in under 3 seconds.

4

📊 Smart Response with Actionable Intel

Returns ranked results with: price benchmarking (vs. 90-day market), availability score, alternative part recommendations, and a one-tap "Contact Supplier" action.

5

🔄 Continuous Learning Loop

Every interaction trains the model. Pricing trends, supplier reliability, seasonal demand — AeroBrain gets smarter with every query, preserving institutional knowledge forever.
